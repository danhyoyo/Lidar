\vspace{0.5em}\noindent
\textbf{Background and Motivation:}\\
Three-dimensional LiDAR perception is fundamental for autonomous navigation, but mainstream detectors rely on heavy point encoders or 3D convolutions (4.0--63.8M parameters), exceeding edge budgets. On-road vehicular safety decisions operate predominantly on the Special Euclidean manifold $SE(2)$. These results demonstrate that deterministic statistical encoding coupled with oriented geometric alignment enables accurate, real-time perception on edge vehicular platforms.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent
\textbf{Core Contributions and Findings:}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.1em}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
    \item \textbf{Deterministic Representation:} Strata-BEV14 rasterizes 14 statistical BEV channels (vertical densities, elevation moments, subcell centroid offsets) in $20.94\,\text{ms}$ on CPU without learned encoders.
    \item \textbf{Compact Backbone with 3D Energy Attention:} MobilePIXORNeXt-S integrates $7 \times 7$ depthwise convolutions with parameter-free Block-SimAM 3D energy attention and a Scale-Gated Feature Pyramid Network with decoupled grouped heads.
    \item \textbf{Physical Augmentation and Geometric Alignment:} Physics-Consistent Ground-Truth Augmentation (PC-GTA) prevents unphysical synthesis ($+6.03\,\text{pp}$ mAP), while Oriented Geometric Alignment (OGA) and IoU-Aware Quality Alignment (IQA) supervise rotated bounding box overlaps ($+3.20\,\text{pp}$ mAP).
    \item \textbf{Empirical Milestones:} With $753{,}569$ parameters ($<0.76\,\text{M}$), MobilePIXORNeXt achieves $71.46\%$ Moderate mAP on KITTI, exceeding PointPillars by $1.33\,\text{pp}$ with an $84.41\%$ parameter reduction, running in $60.96\,\text{ms}$ ($16.41\,\text{FPS}$) on Tesla T4 GPU.
\end{itemize}
