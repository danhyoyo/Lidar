We presented MobilePIXORNeXt, an ultra-compact, proposal-free LiDAR object detector optimizing oriented bounding footprints on the $SE(2)$ manifold for real-time edge perception on resource-constrained platforms. The deterministic Strata-BEV14 rasterizer replaced learned PointNet pillar encoders and 3D sparse convolutions. It preserved continuous vertical distribution moments, material surface reflectivity, and horizontal subcell centroid displacements without learned parameters. A single-branch ConvNeXt-style backbone with parameter-free Block-SimAM 3D energy attention processed multi-scale spatial features. A Scale-Gated Feature Pyramid Network fused these features for decoupled grouped prediction towers. Training utilized Physics-Consistent Ground-Truth Augmentation (PC-GTA), Oriented Geometric Alignment (OGA), and IoU-Aware Quality Alignment (IQA), numerically balanced by Temperature-Softmax Bounded Uncertainty Weighting (T-SBUW). For resource-critical scenarios, Quality-Coupled OGA (Q-OGA) was formulated to achieve head-free confidence calibration with zero parameter overhead.

Evaluation on the KITTI validation benchmark using Moderate BEV AP $R_{40}$ demonstrated a favorable trade-off between detection accuracy, model size, and latency. With $753{,}569$ parameters ($9.34\,\text{MB}$ checkpoint), MobilePIXORNeXt achieved $71.46\%$ Moderate mAP and $73.42\%$ $\text{mAP}_9$ across Car, Pedestrian, and Cyclist. It exceeded PointPillars by $1.33\,\text{pp}$ Moderate mAP with an $84.4\%$ parameter reduction and trailed SECOND ($72.09\%$) by only $0.63\,\text{pp}$ despite using $7.07\times$ fewer parameters ($85.85\%$ reduction). Pedestrian Moderate AP reached $60.75\%$, exceeding published reference values for PointPillars by $+4.74\,\text{pp}$ and SECOND by $+4.19\,\text{pp}$ ($+4.92\,\text{pp}$ and $+4.28\,\text{pp}$ under unified local evaluation). On an NVIDIA Tesla T4 GPU, MobilePIXORNeXt's complete input-to-detection pipeline executed in $60.96\,\text{ms}$ ($16.41\,\text{FPS}$), the lowest latency among the evaluated real-time detectors. In controlled ablations, PC-GTA improved Moderate mAP by $+6.03\,\text{pp}$, while OGA and explicit IQA contributed $+3.20\,\text{pp}$ through rotated geometric overlap alignment and confidence score calibration. Alternatively, head-free Q-OGA reached $69.20\%$ Moderate mAP while saving $18{,}626$ parameters ($+1.82\,\text{pp}$ over baseline Smooth-L1). These empirical milestones demonstrate that high-precision edge LiDAR perception is achieved most effectively through an integrated co-design of deterministic statistical encoding, physically regularized data augmentation, and rotation-aware geometric supervision, rather than relying on parameter-heavy learned encoders or dense 3D sparse convolutions.

Several physical and operational boundaries remain. The primary detector is formulated for oriented 2D Bird's-Eye View footprint localization on the $SE(2)$ manifold, directly addressing collision avoidance clearance and Time-to-Collision margins. While an auxiliary branch supports vertical elevation and height regression, localization on steep vertical grades, multi-level interchanges, and irregular non-planar terrain requires systematic off-road validation. In addition, the present benchmark utilizes 64-beam mechanical rotating LiDAR under fair-weather conditions; adapting Strata-BEV14 to ultra-sparse solid-state sensors (e.g., 16-beam or non-repetitive scan patterns) and adverse weather precipitation remains an open challenge. Future work will investigate range-conditioned scale gating (RC-SGFPN) with continuous Fourier frequency embeddings to compensate for radial point density decay, along with hardware-tailored TensorRT INT8 quantization for low-power micro-mobility microcontrollers.
