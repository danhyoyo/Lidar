We presented MobilePIXORNeXt, a compact proposal-free detector of oriented BEV footprints. Strata-BEV14 summarizes point-cloud densities, elevation statistics, reflectance, and subcell centroid offsets without learned point encoders. A ConvNeXt-style backbone, Block-SimAM attention, scale-gated feature fusion, and grouped heads predict horizontal object geometry. Training combines PC-GTA augmentation, OGA geometric supervision, IQA quality supervision, and bounded multi-task weighting.On the KITTI validation set, MobilePIXORNeXt achieves $71.50\%$ Moderate mAP and $73.45\%$ $\text{mAP}_9$ with an offline pipeline latency of $60.96\,\text{ms}$ ($16.41\,\text{FPS}$), using $753{,}569$ deployable parameters and 12 additional training-only logits. Its parameter count is $84.41\%$ below PointPillars and $85.85\%$ below SECOND. Under the same unified configuration, the OGA--IQA factorial study improves Moderate mAP from $67.38\%$ to $70.58\%$, while the Q-OGA variant reaches $69.20\%$ without a dedicated quality head. These results confirm the effectiveness of combining statistical BEV encoding with joint geometric and quality supervision in a compact detector.The current evaluation focuses on horizontal footprints rather than full 3D cuboids, directed heading, tracking, or closed-loop collision avoidance. In addition, local and reference evaluator outputs have not been shown equivalent, and repeated multi-seed runs are still needed to rigorously quantify initialization sensitivity. Furthermore, validation relies on KITTI's 64-beam LiDAR, leaving sparse sensors, adverse weather, non-planar roads, and embedded deployment untested. Future work will establish a standardized reference-evaluation protocol, evaluate multi-seed stability, and investigate hardware-specific quantization on embedded accelerators.
