Compact LiDAR perception requires balancing geometric accuracy and input-to-detection latency. We present MobilePIXORNeXt, a proposal-free detector of oriented bird's-eye-view (BEV) footprints with 753,569 deployable parameters and 12 additional training-only loss-balancing logits. A deterministic 14-channel Strata-BEV14 rasterizer summarizes vertical densities, elevation statistics, reflectance, and horizontal subcell centroid offsets. A ConvNeXt-style backbone with parameter-free Block-SimAM attention and a scale-gated feature pyramid supplies grouped prediction heads. Training combines physics-consistent ground-truth augmentation, Oriented Geometric Alignment (OGA), and localization-quality supervision. On the KITTI validation split, the seed-42 checkpoint achieves 71.50\% Moderate mean Average Precision (mAP), 73.45\% mean AP across nine class--difficulty cells, and 60.78\% Pedestrian Moderate AP in evaluation set using a local loader-aligned evaluator with 40-point interpolation. The detector has 84.41\% fewer parameters than the evaluated PointPillars model and 85.85\% fewer than SECOND. A separate NVIDIA Tesla T4 profiling snapshot reports 60.96 milliseconds per frame (16.41 frames per second) for sequential offline input preparation, transfer, inference, and post-processing. The T4 snapshot records 71.46\% Moderate mAP and 73.42\% nine-cell mAP. These snapshots are reported separately because evaluator equivalence and checkpoint identity across reports require verification. Historical experiments also examine head-free Q-OGA, which reaches 69.20\% Moderate mAP on an earlier architecture. The results support deterministic statistical encoding as a compact BEV detection approach; validation on embedded hardware remains future work.
