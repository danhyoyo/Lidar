\begin{abstract}
Compact LiDAR perception requires balancing spatial representation, oriented localization, and detection confidence. We present MobilePixorNeXt, a detector combining a fourteen-channel bird's eye view representation, large-kernel depthwise residual blocks, scale-gated fusion, grouped heads, and an oriented geometry objective. Localization quality uses a detached projection-based geometric similarity, with focal context and residual spatial attention for feature refinement. A factorial study on the KITTI validation split examines interactions between geometry and a separate quality head. Under local bird's eye view evaluation, the quality head improves Moderate mean average precision by 2.93 percentage points with the baseline objective and 1.61 points with the geometry objective, indicating overlapping benefits. Matched hundred-epoch training without a separate quality head yields 70.52\% with Q-OGA versus 69.69\% with OGA, using 815,744 detector parameters. The strongest fifty-epoch variant achieves 70.99\% with 852,930 parameters. A separately trained three-dimensional variant shares an initial convolution within each task group and predicts vertical location and height. With 797,382 parameters, it achieves 71.09\% bird's eye view and 57.75\% three-dimensional mean average precision using the pinned reference evaluator. Conditional geometric diagnostics identify vertical location error as a major source of failed car overlaps. These single-seed results motivate joint consideration of geometry, confidence, and vertical accuracy; local ablation scores and reference scores are reported under their respective protocols.
\end{abstract}
