Three-dimensional object detection from Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR) point clouds is critical for autonomous navigation; however, conventional volumetric and pillar-based architectures impose severe computational challenges on edge accelerators. We propose MobilePIXORNeXt, an ultra-compact, proposal-free Bird's-Eye View (BEV) detector operating under 753,569 parameters. The framework replaces learned point encoders with Strata-BEV14, a parameter-free deterministic rasterizer that extracts 14 statistical feature channels---capturing stratified height distributions, elevation moments, surface reflectance, and continuous subcell centroid displacement offsets---in under 21 milliseconds. Spatial features are extracted by a single-branch ConvNeXt-style backbone with embedded parameter-free 3D spatial-energy attention (Block-SimAM), fused through a Scale-Gated Feature Pyramid Network, and predicted by decoupled grouped heads. Training is supervised by Physics-Consistent Ground-Truth Augmentation and Oriented Geometric Alignment, dynamically balanced via 12-parameter Temperature-Softmax Bounded Uncertainty Weighting. On the KITTI benchmark ($R_{40}$), MobilePIXORNeXt achieves 71.46\% Moderate mean Average Precision (mAP) and 73.42\% overall 9-cell mAP across Car, Pedestrian, and Cyclist categories. It outperforms PointPillars (70.13\% mAP) with an 84.4\% parameter reduction and matches SECOND (72.09\% mAP) with 85.8\% fewer parameters, while reaching 60.75\% Moderate AP on pedestrians. The complete pipeline executes in 60.96 milliseconds (16.41 frames per second) on an NVIDIA Tesla T4 GPU, delivering the fastest end-to-end throughput among evaluated real-time detectors. Our findings demonstrate that deterministic statistical encoding and oriented geometric supervision enable high-accuracy LiDAR perception on resource-constrained embedded systems.
