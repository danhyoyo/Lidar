\subsection{Inference cost}

Table~\ref{tab:efficiency} reports the separate runtime-comparison block. Its MobilePIXORNeXt entry records 17.88~ms model-forward latency and 60.96~ms end-to-end latency, corresponding to 55.94 and 16.41~FPS. The difference between those timings includes the pipeline stages outside the model and illustrates why forward FPS is insufficient to describe complete-system throughput.

\input{tables/efficiency}

The final ablation row instead records 8.67~ms model-forward and 42.16~ms end-to-end latency. These are two benchmark records, not repeated measurements known to share the same conditions. They are not averaged, and the faster record is not combined with the comparison table's accuracy to construct a new operating point. Hardware, precision, preprocessing and postprocessing implementations, and timing boundaries remain to be attached to each record.

Table~\ref{tab:components} separates complete configurations in panel (a) from their results and source records in panel (b). The backbone is MobilePIXOR in M0.
