\begin{abstract}
Compact LiDAR perception requires balancing geometric accuracy and input-to-detection latency. We present MobilePIXORNeXt, a proposal-free detector of oriented bird's-eye-view (BEV) footprints with 753,569 deployable parameters and 12 additional training-only loss-balancing logits. A deterministic 14-channel Strata-BEV14 rasterizer summarizes vertical densities, elevation statistics, reflectance, and horizontal subcell centroid offsets. A ConvNeXt-style backbone with parameter-free Block-SimAM attention and a scale-gated feature pyramid supplies grouped prediction heads. Training combines physics-consistent ground-truth augmentation, Oriented Geometric Alignment (OGA), and localization-quality supervision. On the KITTI validation split, the seed-42 checkpoint achieves 71.50\% Moderate mean Average Precision (mAP), 73.45\% mean AP across nine class--difficulty cells, and 60.78\% Pedestrian Moderate AP under standard 40-point interpolated evaluation ($R_{40}$). Across three random seeds, MobilePIXORNeXt delivers consistent performance ($70.94 \pm 0.89\%$ Moderate mAP), achieving a $+30.58\,\text{pp}$ improvement over the MobilePIXOR baseline. The detector has 84.41\% fewer parameters than PointPillars and 85.85\% fewer than SECOND, while outperforming PointPillars by 1.22 points Moderate mAP and remaining within 0.68 points of SECOND. On an NVIDIA L4 GPU, the complete sequential pipeline operates at 37.29\,ms per frame (26.82 FPS), including preprocessing, CPU-to-GPU transfer, inference, and post-processing. Ablation experiments also demonstrate head-free Q-OGA, which reaches 69.09\% Moderate mAP while eliminating the dedicated quality prediction tower. These results demonstrate that deterministic statistical BEV encoding coupled with oriented geometric alignment provides a high-accuracy, resource-efficient solution for edge LiDAR perception.
\end{abstract}
