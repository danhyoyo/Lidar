We presented MobilePIXORNeXt, a compact proposal-free detector of oriented BEV footprints. Strata-BEV14 summarizes point-cloud densities, elevation statistics, reflectance, and subcell centroid offsets without learned point encoders. A ConvNeXt-style backbone, Block-SimAM attention, scale-gated feature fusion, and grouped heads predict horizontal object geometry. Training combines PC-GTA augmentation, OGA geometric supervision, IQA quality supervision, and bounded multi-task weighting.

On the KITTI benchmark, MobilePIXORNeXt achieves $71.50\%$ Moderate mAP and $73.45\%$ $\text{mAP}_9$ ($70.94 \pm 0.89\%$ across three random seeds), using only $753{,}569$ deployable parameters alongside 12 transient training logits. On an NVIDIA L4 GPU, the full sequential pipeline achieves $37.29\,\text{ms}$ latency ($26.82\,\text{FPS}$). With $84.41\%$ fewer parameters than PointPillars and $85.85\%$ fewer than SECOND, MobilePIXORNeXt outperforms PointPillars by $1.22\,\text{pp}$ Moderate mAP. In controlled micro-ablations, our OGA formulation improves Moderate mAP from $66.82\%$ (Smooth-L1 baseline) to $69.50\%$ at 50 epochs ($71.46\%$ under the standard 100-epoch schedule), while head-free Q-OGA achieves $69.09\%$ while saving $18{,}626$ parameters. These results confirm the effectiveness of combining statistical BEV encoding with joint geometric and quality supervision in a compact detector.

The current evaluation focuses on horizontal footprints rather than full 3D cuboids, directed heading, tracking, or closed-loop collision avoidance. While validation relies on KITTI's 64-beam LiDAR, future work will explore adaptation to ultra-sparse LiDAR sensors, severe weather conditions, non-planar road topologies, and deployment on low-power embedded accelerators with TensorRT or INT8 quantization.
