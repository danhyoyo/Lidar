We presented MobilePIXORNeXt, a compact proposal-free detector of oriented BEV footprints. Strata-BEV14 summarizes point-cloud densities, elevation statistics, reflectance, and subcell centroid offsets without learned point encoders. A ConvNeXt-style backbone, Block-SimAM attention, scale-gated feature fusion, and grouped heads predict horizontal object geometry. Training combines PC-GTA augmentation, OGA geometric supervision, IQA quality supervision, and bounded multi-task weighting.

The seed-42 checkpoint achieves $71.50\%$ Moderate mAP and $73.45\%$ $\text{mAP}_9$ in the archived L4 local-evaluator report, using $753{,}569$ deployable parameters and 12 additional training-only logits. Its parameter count is $84.41\%$ below PointPillars and $85.85\%$ below SECOND. A separate T4 snapshot reports $71.46\%$ Moderate mAP, $73.42\%$ $\text{mAP}_9$, and $60.96\,\text{ms}$ offline pipeline latency ($16.41\,\text{FPS}$). The historical OGA--IQA factorial study improves Moderate mAP from $67.38\%$ to $70.58\%$ within its earlier focal-context architecture. Historical Q-OGA reaches $69.20\%$ using that earlier architecture without a dedicated quality head. These experiments motivate compact statistical BEV detection, while their differing configurations limit component-level attribution to the final detector.

The evaluation concerns horizontal footprints rather than full 3D cuboids, directed heading, tracking, or closed-loop collision avoidance. Local and reference evaluator outputs have not been shown equivalent. The available seed runs change training budgets and, for MobilePIXOR, input representations; matched repeated runs are needed to quantify initialization sensitivity. Validation uses KITTI's 64-beam LiDAR, leaving sparse sensors, adverse weather, non-planar roads, and embedded deployment untested. Future work will establish a common reference-evaluation protocol, reproduce controlled component ablations, and evaluate hardware-specific quantization on embedded accelerators.
